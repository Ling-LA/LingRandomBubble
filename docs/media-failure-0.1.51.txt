QQ 9.3.50 / LingRandomBubble 0.1.51 media anomaly comparisons
Recorded 2026-10-03. This is sanitized runtime evidence, not a root-cause proof.

Initial mode ON
- Picture sending showed the reported abnormal behavior.
- Ling held/resumed/cancelled remained 60/60/0; the picture click did not add a
  Ling-held text send. No body, filename, account or conversation ID is recorded.

Mode OFF in the same comparison
- Pictures showed the same abnormal behavior with per-message mode disabled.
- Ordinary text sent successfully with that mode disabled.
- These observations do not prove either QQ or Ling caused the media anomaly.

After an actual cold QQ start, still on 0.1.51
- With mode OFF, a new synthetic JPEG and the same earlier PNG entered history.
- After mode ON was restored, native JPEG sends succeeded directly in both an
  authorized group conversation and an authorized private conversation.
- QFun's mixed text/picture option was OFF when observed and was not changed.
  The comparison cannot attribute the anomaly or recovery to that option.

Receiver evidence
- The receiver sent a screenshot of the group picture through an authorized
  private conversation. That screenshot was actually read by the main executor.
- It supports the observed group picture receipt at that time. No exit/re-entry
  confirmation was obtained. The screenshot and private conversation remain
  excluded from public sources; no nickname, group name or pixels are included.
- This later media observation is separate from 0.1.51's earlier reply test,
  whose receiver confirmation was text-only. It is not a 0.1.52 receiver result.

Limits
- The real cause remains unknown. Mode-OFF failures, no increase in Ling holds,
  and later successes do not establish that Ling, QFun or a specific QQ state is
  responsible. Cold-start recovery alone is not proof of a permanent fix.
- 0.1.52 tightens native-media/old-text-wait separation and requires a confirmed
  empty current QQ photo queue before delaying a text click. Its isolated tests
  and installation do not establish final QQ media delivery or all-media scope.
- Favorite stickers have a separate PicEmotionSendEvent path. A photo queue
  observation does not establish coverage of every image/sticker/attachment.
